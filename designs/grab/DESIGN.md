# Grab

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Grab is Southeast Asia's "everyday everything app": rides, food delivery, groceries, parcels, payments and lending in one green app. The brand is built on one color. Grab Green `#00B14F` is everywhere: the logo, the app icon, the driver jackets, the delivery bags, the primary button. Everything else is white space, near-black text and photography of real riders, drivers and merchants in Singapore, Jakarta, Bangkok, Manila, Kuala Lumpur and Ho Chi Minh City.

What makes it recognisable:

- **One green, used with confidence.** Full-bleed green sections, green pill buttons, green tab underlines. It reads as friendly and practical rather than corporate.
- **The inline wordmark.** The 2023 logo draws "Grab" as a double-stroke line (an outline with a parallel inner stroke). The custom typeface family, Grab Community, has a matching "Inline" cut used for big campaign words.
- **Rounded, chunky headlines.** Headings use Grab Community Solid, a rounded geometric sans with soft terminals. Body text is plain Inter.
- **Service tiles.** The site and app are organised around small service tiles (Rides, Food, Mart, Express, DineOut, Financial services), each with an icon and a one-line description.
- **People-first photography.** Hero images show driver-partners, merchants and families, warm and daylight-lit, usually with a green element in the frame.
- **Moderate density.** Marketing pages breathe; the app itself is dense, with lists of restaurants, promo banners and a bottom tab bar.

Mood words: friendly, local, quick, dependable, upbeat.

## 3. Typography

**Display:** Grab Community Solid, a proprietary rounded sans in Light (300) to ExtraBold (800). Loaded from assets.grab.com. A companion cut, Grab Community Inline, draws the double-line style seen in the logo and campaign headlines.

**Body:** Inter (400, 500, 600, 700), self-hosted. Some older pages still use Roboto and Roboto Slab from the WordPress theme; treat those as legacy.

**Licensing:** Grab Community is not public. Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** (700-800) as the nearest free match for rounded headlines; Nunito is a softer option. Inter is free on Google Fonts.

```css
--font-display: "Grab Community Solid", "Plus Jakarta Sans", "Inter", sans-serif;
--font-body: "Inter", system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif;
```

### Rules

- Sentence case for headings ("Grab. Making every day better" style), Title Case only for product names (GrabFood, GrabMart, GrabExpress).
- Product names are one word with camel case: GrabFood, GrabPay, GrabCoins. Never "Grab Food".
- Headlines are short and often end with a period.
- Prices and ETAs use Inter 600 with tabular numerals.

## 4. Logo & Brand Mark

The logo is the word "Grab" in a custom geometric letterform drawn as an inline: each stroke is an outline with a parallel inner line, so the letters look like two-lane roads. It ships in Grab Green on white, or white on green.

- **App icon:** the white inline wordmark centred on a solid Grab Green square.
- **Color versions:** green on white (primary), white on green, white on photography with a dark scrim, single-color black for print only.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the "a" on all sides (approximate).
- **Minimum size:** about 64px wide on screen; below that the inline gap closes up, so switch to a solid version.

Misuse:

- Do not fill the gap between the inline strokes or redraw it as a solid word.
- Do not recolor the logo in accent colors (orange, lime) or apply gradients.
- Do not place green on green photography without a white container.
- Do not set "Grab" in Inter or any other font as a logo substitute.

The home page and app open on a grid of service tiles:

- 4 columns on mobile (app), 3-6 on desktop.
- Each tile: 56-64px rounded icon on a light tint (`#CCEFDC` or white with border), label 14px/600 `#161A1D` below, optional description 14px `#697078`.
- On grab.com the "Consumer / Driver / Merchant / Enterprise" mega-menu lists services as title (16px/600) plus one-line description (14px muted).

### Cards

- White, radius 16px, `box-shadow: 0 5px 15px rgba(0,0,0,.08)`; on hover `0 14px 25px rgba(0,0,0,.16)` with a 2-4px lift.
- Image on top (16:9 or 4:3, radius 16px on top corners), 20-24px padding, title 18-20px/700 display font, body 14-16px Inter.
- Featured card variant: solid Grab Green fill, white text, same shadow.
- Badge in the top-right corner: 22px tall, green fill, white uppercase 14px text, 2px radius.

### Navigation

- White header, about 72px tall (approximate), green logo on the left.
- Top-level items: "About", "Consumer", "Driver", "Merchant", "Enterprise", then a search icon and a country/language switcher.
- Hover opens a full-width mega-menu with two-line entries (title + description).
- Active tab: text `#161A1D`, 2-3px `#00B14F` underline.

- Underline tabs: inactive `#697078`, active `#161A1D` with green border-bottom.
- Pill subnav (filters, cuisines): inactive `#F5F5F5` fill and `#383E43` text, active `#00B14F` fill and white text, radius 9999px, padding 6px 14px.

- Checkboxes and radios: checked fill `#00B14F`.
- Location input in the app: pin icon left, grey `#F5F5F5` fill, radius 8px, "Where to?" placeholder.

### Badges and labels

- Count badge: 18px circle, `#00B14F`, white 11px text.
- Promo label: uppercase 12-14px, padding 0 10px, 2px radius, green or coral fill.
- Rating chip: star icon in `#F7C942` + 14px/600 number.

### App patterns

- Bottom tab bar: Home, Activity, Payment, Messages, Account. Active icon and label in `#00B14F`.
- Restaurant list rows: 72-88px square thumbnail with 8px radius, name 16px/600, cuisine and distance 14px muted, promo tag in orange or green.

### Modals

White, radius 16px, max width 480-560px, 24-32px padding, scrim `rgba(22,26,29,.5)`. Close icon top-right in `#161A1D`.

## 6. Layout & Spacing

- **Spacing:** 4px base. Common steps: 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96px.
- **Content width:** about 1200px container with 24px gutters (approximate; UIkit container is 1200px).
- **Breakpoints:** UIkit defaults, 640, 960, 1200, 1600px.
- **Section spacing:** 64-96px on desktop, 40-48px on mobile.
- **Grid:** 12 columns on desktop; service tiles snap to 3, 4 or 6 columns.
- **Density:** marketing pages are airy; the app is compact with 16px side gutters and 12px gaps between cards.

## 7. Depth, Elevation & Motion

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Card | `0 5px 15px rgba(0,0,0,.08)` | Default card |
| Card hover | `0 14px 25px rgba(0,0,0,.16)` | Hovered card |
| Large | `0 28px 50px rgba(0,0,0,.16)` | Hero device mockups |
| Small | `0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,.08)` | Chips, dropdowns |
| Floating CTA | `4px 6px 12px 0 rgba(28,28,28,.3)` | Floating pill button |
| Sticky bar | `0 -12px 24px rgba(28,28,28,.1)` | Bottom sheets |

- Borders are light (`#E5E9F0`); shadows do most of the separation.
- Motion is brisk: 150-250ms ease-out for hovers, 300ms for menus. Progress bars animate width over 600ms.
- Scroll-in fades (Elementor `fadeIn`) on marketing sections; keep them subtle and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 8. Iconography & Imagery

- **Icons:** rounded, filled glyphs with soft corners, usually two-tone (green plus a light green or white). Line icons are 1.5-2px stroke with rounded caps. Service icons are larger illustrated objects (a car, a bowl of noodles, a parcel) in flat color.
- **Photography:** real driver-partners, merchants and customers across Southeast Asia, natural daylight, candid, often with the green helmet, jacket or delivery bag visible.
- **Illustration:** flat, friendly vector scenes in green, mint, orange and sunflower, with simple characters.
- **Maps:** light grey map tiles with a green route line and a green or dark pin.

## 9. Voice & Microcopy

Warm, simple and local. Grab speaks to riders, drivers and merchants in plain English (and in local languages per market), and keeps product names front and centre.

- Sentence case, short sentences, one idea each.
- Talk about the person: "you", "your family", "your business".
- Name the service: "GrabFood", "GrabMart", "GrabExpress".
- CTAs are verbs: "Book a ride", "Order food", "Drive with us", "Partner with us", "Download the app".

Sample strings:

- Page title: "Grab. The Everyday Everything App"
- Nav descriptions: "Everything to do with getting you from A to B", "Send things easily and quickly", "Be your own boss and drive on your own time"
- Rides empty state: "Where to?"
- Food card: "Free delivery", "Promo", "20-30 min"
- Rewards: "Earn GrabCoins every time you pay"

## 10. Do's and Don'ts

**Do**

- Use Grab Green `#00B14F` as the single primary action color.
- Use pill buttons with a 56px radius and Inter 600 labels.
- Set headlines in a rounded geometric display face, body in Inter.
- Organise pages around service tiles and short two-line menu entries.
- Show real people and places from Southeast Asia.
- Use mint `#CCEFDC` and teal tint `#EEF9F9` for soft backgrounds.

**Don't**

- Don't introduce a second brand color for actions (no blue links).
- Don't use thin, high-contrast serif headlines; the brand is rounded and friendly.
- Don't put the inline logo on busy photography.
- Don't split product names ("Grab Food").
- Don't use heavy dark shadows; keep them soft and short.
